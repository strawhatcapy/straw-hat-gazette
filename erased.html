Erased Review

Here is my review of the popular anime, Erased. The anime is a mystery series with slight horror and many psychological components to the story. The anime is based on a manga series written and illustrated by Kei Sanbe and was active from 2012 to 2016. After the manga series ended, in short succession the anime series was released in 2016 and the live action movie was released in 2017.

Erased is a unique anime series that follows the heroics of Satoru Fujinima who was given a priceless chance to change the past. In 1988, 3 children [Sugita Hiromi 11 yrs, Nakanishi Aya 11 yrs, and Hinazuki Kayo 10 yrs] were kidnapped and murdered by a serial killer. The protagonist at the time was 11 yrs old and attended the same school as Hinazuki Kayo and Sugita Hiromi, a close friend of Satoru.

The beginning of the anime shows a 29 year old Satoru Fujinima who works as a pizza delivery man and aspires to be a reputable manga artist. Apart from his surprisingly normal life, Satoru has the unique ability to go back in time before a tragedy occurs {he does not have much control of his powers}. He has saved countless lives, though he does not receive any recognition since he likes to keep his powers a secret.


Example of Satoru's Powers

One day, Satoru is on the road delivering pizzas when his powers suddenly kick in and rewinds time by 5 seconds. He figures out that a truck driver was unconscious and the truck would be bound to hit a little boy crossing the street. Satoru swiftly moves beside the truck to steer the truck away from the boy, and succeeds. The truck moves into the opposite lane and crashes into a car.


Hinazuki Kayo

Hinazuki Kayo is one of the main characters in the series, Erased. She was the first victim of the serial killer in the 1988 case and was a classmate of Satoru Fujinima. Satoru felt like he could have saved her if he had not left her alone in the past. She is displayed initially with bruises on her thigh which explores that she gets abused regularly.

  • Her abuse explains why she behaves the way she does [shy and distant].
  • She was an ideal target for the serial killer because she always stayed alone.
  • She gains more confidence once she realizes she has friends who are there for her.
  • Her story was very sad and explored the theme of abuse and its impact on individuals.
  • She wants to be separated from her mother, Akemi because of the abuse she experiences.
  • She lives happily after getting separated from her mother and living with her grandmother.
  • After Satoru's coma, she marries Sugita Hiromi and starts a new life.

COMMON CRITIQUES of Hinazuki Kayo

I believe it makes perfect sense that Hinazuki Kayo would not wait 15 years to marry Satoru, because even though Satoru was unconscious for that time, Hinazuki Kayo would have made new memories and moved on with her life. That's just how life is, and Kei Sanbe gave a very realistic closure to the story of Hinazuki Kayo and Satoru Fujinima.

Overall Thoughts

The setting of the story was good, a classic city with a mountain and a normal city. The anime did a poor job in developing character relationships, due to shrinking the content to only 12 episodes. The anime's pacing was not properly executed (e.g. The first 6 episodes were equivalent to 11 chapters of the manga and the next 6 were equivalent to a whopping 33 chapters). The anime's biggest flaw in my opinion, was that the serial killer was very obvious to guess. I got it after the first time I saw him, because they showed his face shape and hair, which was way too similar to Gaku Yashiro. To add, there were many hints that he was the serial killer, which made it very easy to guess. Hinazuki Kayo's character was very well-written and she was my second favourite character in the series. Satoru's mom is a great character and a great mother, she was supportive of Satoru and took care of him well. The killer, Gaku Yashiro, was poorly written in the final episodes of the anime because his motives were not explained well. Why would he try to kill Satoru, then save him, then try to kill him again? It was very confusing. We also did not know how Satoru was saved from what seemed like a definite death inside a locked car underwater. The anime just transitions to Sachiko taking care of him when he is in a coma. Despite the anime's flaws, I would still highly recommend it because of its emotional side to the story rather than the mystery. I definitely shed a tear or two while watching the anime.

Final Rating

3.16/5
